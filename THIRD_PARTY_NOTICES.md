## 3. External programs invoked as separate processes / Programas externos

**Quantum ESPRESSO** (`pw.x`, `ph.x`, `dos.x`, `projwfc.x`, `bands.x`, `pp.x`,
`epsilon.x`, `q2r.x`, `matdyn.x`, `dynmat.x`, `hp.x`, `neb.x`, `ld1.x`,
`pwcond.x`, `pw2wannier90.x`, `turbo_lanczos.x`, `turbo_davidson.x`,
`xspectra.x`) — GPL-2.0-or-later. Olla-DFT does not bundle, link or modify
Quantum ESPRESSO: it writes its input files and runs the binaries found on
the system.

**Pseudopotentials** (UPF files) used for calculations are provided by the user and keep their own licenses. The source/test archive includes one project-generated fixture, `tests/datos/Si.hueco1s.UPF.gz`: its embedded generation input identifies author `QEkit`, QE atomic 6.6, and date 29Aug2026. It is a test fixture, not a general-purpose pseudopotential library; it retains the existing project-example GPL-3.0-or-later license.
